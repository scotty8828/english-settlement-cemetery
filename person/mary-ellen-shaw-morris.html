Biography

Mary Ellen Shaw was born 17 April 1853 in Rome Township, a daughter of William Shaw (1815–1890) and Jane Gregory Shaw (1815–1895), both already documented on this site. She had at least one sister already documented here, Jane Ann Shaw Shaw (1848–1931, page pending), along with three other siblings not currently found in the cemetery's own record: Sarah Shaw Hasbrouck (1844–1908), Catherine Shaw Hasbrouck (1847–1929), and Martha Shaw Harrison (1851–1890).

In the fall of 1873, Mary Ellen married Inskip Morris (1849–1930, page pending on this site) — a brother of Benjamin Morris, already documented on this site. Together they had seven children, one of whom, Inskip Morris Jr. (1894–1901), died in early childhood. The other six were Lucy Morris Stoke (1874–1951), Warren Inskip Morris (1876–1960), Anna Morris Dailey (1879–1954), Clifton Morris (1882–1964), William E. "Will" Morris (1885–1966), and David Roy Morris (1890–1964). By the time of her death, her obituary counted thirty-three grandchildren and thirteen great-grandchildren.

Two more names sometimes appear alongside Mary Ellen's children in family records: Reuben Morris (1875–1917) and Lulu Martha Hicks Morris (1888–1914), both already documented on this site. Reuben was Mary Ellen's son-in-law, married to her daughter Anna; Lulu was her daughter-in-law, the first wife of her son William E. Morris. Neither was Mary Ellen's biological child.

Mary Ellen died suddenly on the morning of 15 June 1922. She had not been in good health for some time but had remained up and attending to household duties. She retired as usual the evening before; the next morning her husband found her in considerable pain, complaining of pains in her chest. After he gave her remedies kept in the house and she seemed easier, he went out to the barn to do the morning chores — and returned to find she had died, apparently without a struggle. Her death certificate lists the cause as probable acute myocarditis. She was a member of Sparta Grange, and her obituary described her as "a kind and loving mother, a faithful wife and an obliging and courteous neighbor and friend."

Primary source — obituary, Titusville Herald, 16 June 1922, p. 10

"MRS INSKIP MORRIS IS FOUND DEAD IN HER BED — Well Known Rome Township Lady, While in Failing Health, Was Ill But Short Time — The large circle of acquaintances of Mrs Mary Ellen Morris, wife of Inskip Morris, were shocked yesterday to learn of her death, which occurred suddenly at about 6 am yesterday at her home in Rome township, near Vrooman. While Mrs Morris had not been in good health for some time, she had been up and around attending to the household duties. She retired with Mr Morris as usual on Wednesday evening and upon arising yesterday morning, Mr Morris noticed that his wife was suffering considerable pain. She complained of pains in her breast and after giving her remedies which were kept in the house, Mrs Morris became easier and her husband went to the barn to do the chores. When he returned he found her dead, she having passed away apparently without a struggle.

Mrs Morris was the daughter of the late William and Jane Shaw and was born in Rome township on April 17, 1853. She grew to womanhood at the Shaw home and in the fall of 1873 was united in marriage with Mr Morris. To them were born seven children, one of whom died in early childhood. The surviving children are: Warren Morris, who resides on a farm in Warren county, Mrs Charles Stoke of Tryonville, Mrs Anna Morris of Meadville, Clift Morris of Buell Corners, William Morris, who resides near Vrooman and David Morris, now living in the west. There are thirty-three grandchildren and thirteen great-grandchildren, besides two sisters, Mrs David Hasbrouck and Miss Jane Shaw, both residing near Vrooman. Mrs Morris was a member of Sparta Grange and was held in high esteem by all who knew her as a kind and loving mother, a faithful wife and an obliging and courteous neighbor and friend."

— Titusville Herald, 16 June 1922, p. 10, as reproduced on Find A Grave

Monument
A Find A Grave photograph (contributed by Virginia Bates-Hulings) shows a shared monument with her husband Inskip Morris, inscribed "INSKIP SON OF RICHARD & JANE HARRISON MORRIS, BORN DEC. 28, 1848, DIED JULY 11, 1930, AGED 80 YR 6 MO 13 DA" and, beneath it, "MARY HELEN SHAW HIS WIFE, BORN APR 17, 1853, DIED JUNE 15, 1922, AGED 69 YR 1 MO 28 DA." The stone's own inscription gives his birth year as 1848 and her age at death as 68 days short of the certificate's count — both minor discrepancies with the documented dates used on this page, noted rather than resolved.
