Biography

Jane Harrison was born in England on 27 January 1818, the eldest known child of Benjamin Inskip Harrison and his first wife, Nancy Brown Harrison. Through her father she was a granddaughter of Benjamin Harrison and Jane Inskip Harrison, the English-born founders of the Harrison family at the English Settlement. Her father was a brother to Jane Harrison (1806–1887), who married Richard Morris — making this younger Jane the niece of her aunt of the same name, one generation apart, and both women marrying into the Morris family in turn.

Jane's family emigrated from England sometime in the 1820s. A younger brother, Benjamin Harrison, was born in 1822 with no American birthplace yet recorded, while another brother, Thomas Harrison, was born in 1829 in Paterson, Passaic County, New Jersey — placing the family's crossing sometime in the mid-1820s, part of the same wave of Lancashire and Yorkshire textile emigration that brought other branches of the Harrison family to America. The family eventually made its way west to Crawford County, Pennsylvania, joining the community already being built by Jane's grandmother, Jane Inskip Harrison, and her extended family.

Jane married William "Billie" Morris Sr. (1811–1890), a brother to Richard Morris (1810–1871) — the same Richard Morris who had married Jane's aunt, Jane Harrison, in 1837. Both William and Richard were sons of James Morris (1782–1858), the Lancashire blacksmith whose imprisonment following an 1819 pikehead conviction and eventual emigration is documented elsewhere on this site. Aunt and niece each married one of James Morris's sons, doubling the tie between the Harrison and Morris families in a single generation. The exact date of Jane and William's own marriage has not yet been found, but their first child was born in 1837, setting a latest possible date. Together they raised at least eight children at Rome Township between 1837 and 1855, spanning the years leading up to and through the Civil War.

Jane Harrison Morris died in 1885 at approximately 66 or 67 years of age; the exact date of her death has not yet been documented. She is buried at the English Settlement beneath a shared monument with her husband, who outlived her by five years and died in 1890.

Death date incomplete: Only the year 1885 is currently documented for Jane's death; Find A Grave itself lists her age as "66–67," reflecting the same gap. No obituary or death record has yet been located.
Marriage date unconfirmed: No marriage record for Jane and William Morris Sr. has yet been found. The birth of their first child in 1837 sets only a latest possible date.
John Brown Morris and his cousin, John Morris: Jane and William's son John Brown Morris (1855–1926) was first cousin to John Morris, son of Richard Morris and Jane's aunt Jane Harrison (1806–1887) — the two men behind the long-flagged Find A Grave / Ancestry confusion elsewhere in this project, worsened by the fact that both cousins married women named Seraph. The master record's "John Brown Morris (1845–1928)" entry (slug john-brown-morris-1845) is believed to actually be John Morris, Richard and Jane's son; the "Brown" appears to be an error carried over from his cousin's record. Jane and William's own son is the entry at slug john-brown-morris-1855, correctly named. To be confirmed and untangled when those pages are built.
Morris family link confirmed: William "Billie" Morris Sr. and Richard Morris (1810–1871, husband of Jane's aunt Jane Harrison) are brothers, both sons of James Morris (1782–1858). Confirmed by Scott Morris.
Sister also married a Morris: Jane's sister Ellen Harrison Morris (1836–1906) also appears to have married into the Morris family. Whether this is the same James Morris branch as William Morris Sr. and Richard Morris is unconfirmed and worth checking once Ellen's own page is built.
Samuel Morris link unconfirmed: Find A Grave lists a son, Samuel Morris, who died in 1853. This may correspond to the "Samuel Morris" (dates unknown) already present in the master cemetery record, but the connection has not been confirmed.
