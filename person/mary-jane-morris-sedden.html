Biography

Mary Jane Morris was born 7 March 1846 in Pittsburgh, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, a daughter of William "Billie" Morris Sr. (1811–1890) and Jane Harrison Morris (1818–1885), both already documented on this site. She came to Crawford County with her parents while still a small child. Her father, an English immigrant who had learned the machinist trade in Paterson, New Jersey and later worked at it in Pittsburgh, bought a partly-cleared hundred-acre farm in Rome Township in 1848 — the old Vroman farm — paying for it over time with wages earned at his trade while his eldest son ran the farm at home. Practically all of Mary Jane's long life was spent in Rome Township, where, her 1930 obituary notes, she was held in high esteem by a wide circle of acquaintances and was for many years a member of the Vrooman United Presbyterian Church.

She was one of at least eight children: William S. Morris Jr. (1837–1915), Sarah Ann Morris Myers (1839–1906), Nancy Elizabeth Morris Magee (1844–1874), James L. "Jimmy" Morris (1848–1921), Benjamin Harrison Morris (1852–1901), John Brown Morris (1855–1926), and Samuel Morris (died young, 1853), all already documented on this site except Sarah Ann and Nancy Elizabeth, who are not buried here.

On 2 September 1867 Mary Jane married Thomas Sedden (1845–1914), already documented on this site. They had two children: William Truble Sedden (1868–1886, already documented on this site), who died at 17, and Ida Leona Sedden Harrison (1870–1957), who married E. R. Harrison and is not buried here. Thomas Sedden died 2 May 1914, leaving Mary Jane a widow for the last sixteen years of her life.

By 1929 Mary Jane's daughter Ida was herself a widow — her husband E. R. Harrison having died 1 October that year — and mother and daughter moved together to Titusville early that October. Mary Jane Morris Sedden died 5 January 1930 at 3:15 in the morning at Ida's home, 208 North Washington Street, Titusville, following an attack of pneumonia, though she had been failing in health for a year or more. Her death certificate records the cause as chronic myocarditis, with bronchitis and pneumonia as contributory, of three days' duration. She was 83. Funeral services were held from her daughter's home, Rev. Dr. F. M. Redinger of the First M. E. Church officiating, with interment following at Harrison Cemetery near Vrooman.

Obituary
Titusville Herald · 6 January 1930, p. 5

PIONEER RESIDENT OF ROME TOWNSHIP DIES — Mrs Mary Jane Sedden Passes Away at Home of Daughter, Mrs Ida Harrison, Early Sunday — Mrs Mary Jane Sedden, widow of Thomas Sedden, and pioneer resident of Rome township, passed away at 3:15 o'clock, Sunday morning at the home of her daughter, Mrs Ida Harrison, 208 North Washington street, following an attack of pneumonia, although she had been failing in health for the past year or more. Mrs Sedden was a daughter of the late Mr and Mrs William Morris, pioneer residents of Rome township. She was born in Pittsburgh on March 7, 1846, and came to Crawford county with her parents when a small child. Practically all of her long life was spent in Rome township and she was held in high esteem by a wide circle of acquaintances. For many years she was a member of the Vrooman United Presbyterian Church. On September 2, 1867, the deceased was united in marriage with Thomas Sedden, who passed away on May 2, 1914. To them were born two children, William Truble Sedden, who died at the age of 17 years, and Mrs Ida Harrison, widow of E R Harrison, who died on October 1, last. Mrs Harrison and her mother moved to Titusville early in October. Funeral services in memory of Mrs Sedden will be held from the home of the daughter, 208 North Washington street, Tuesday afternoon at 2 o'clock, Rev Dr F M Redinger, pastor of the First M E church, officiating and interment following in the Harrison cemetery near Vrooman. Friends are kindly asked to omit sending flowers.
