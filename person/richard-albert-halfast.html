Biography

Richard Albert Halfast was born 17 April 1855 in Venango Township, Crawford County, Pennsylvania, the son of Henry H. Halfast, an immigrant from Germany, and Margaret Maria "Peggy" Homan Halfast. He had at least three siblings: John Henry Halfast (1865–1942), Edward William Halfast (1869–1938), and Leslie Monroe Halfast (1876–1958). Like his father before him, Richard worked as a farmer.

Richard's first marriage was to Sarah Ann Coon. A son, Richard Henry Halfast, was born to them in 1884. The marriage ended in divorce, finalized in 1893, according to the record filed the following year when Richard remarried.

On 12 November 1894, in Spartansburg, Crawford County, Richard married Rosina Harrison — already documented on this site — recorded on their marriage license as divorced and single, respectively. Rosina was the daughter of William Harrison and Sarah Harrison Harrison, also documented on this site, and the sister of Mary Ann Harrison Hasson, wife of John M. Hasson, whose own biography appears here as well. This marriage placed Richard within the same extended Harrison family already connected through several other pages on this site.

Richard Albert Halfast died suddenly on Christmas Day, 1907, at Rome Township. The attending physician, Dr. Waggoner of Spartansburg, found him already dead on arrival and recorded the probable cause as cerebral hemorrhage; no autopsy was performed. His brother, Leslie, served as informant on the death certificate. Richard was buried two days later, on 27 December, at what his own death certificate records as "English Settlement" — a further primary-source confirmation of the cemetery's founding name, this one from the end of 1907.

First marriage, divorce documented plainly: The 1894 marriage license record itself states Richard's prior marital status as "divorced," with the divorce dated 1893. His son Richard Henry (b. 1884) was born during that first marriage, to Sarah Ann Coon.
Extends the Harrison marriage network further: Rosina Harrison, Richard's second wife, was the daughter of William Harrison and Sarah Harrison Harrison — themselves first cousins, as documented on John M. Hasson's page — and the sister of Mary Ann Harrison Hasson, John M. Hasson's wife. Richard Halfast and John M. Hasson each married a daughter of the same couple.
"English Settlement" confirmed again, late in 1907: Richard's own death certificate records his place of burial simply as "English Settlement" — a further primary-source instance of the cemetery's founding name still in official use.
Parents and siblings not yet confirmed buried here: Henry H. Halfast, Margaret Maria Homan Halfast, and Richard's three known siblings are not currently in the cemetery master record; whether any are buried here has not been confirmed.
