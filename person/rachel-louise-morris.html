Biography

Rachel Louise Morris was born 21 August 1917 in Rome Township, Crawford County, a daughter of William E. "Will" Morris (1885–1966) and Treva Coral Henton Morris (1888–1961). She lived just under two months.

Her family history needs a correction from what one Ancestry family tree shows. That tree lists Lulu Martha Hicks Morris (1888–1914, already documented on this site) under "Rachel's parents" — but Lulu died three years before Rachel was born, so she cannot be Rachel's biological mother. Both Rachel's death certificate and her Find A Grave record correctly name Treva Henton as her mother; the tree's display appears to be carrying forward William's first wife by default rather than updating to his second. William E. Morris had in fact been married twice: first to Lulu, who died in 1914 at only twenty-six, and then to Treva. Because Lulu's own children with William — Opal Irene Morris (later Pier, b. 1906), Kenneth W. Morris (b. 1907), and Wayne E. Morris (1911–1911, already documented on this site, who died as an infant) — were all born before Lulu's 1914 death, they are Rachel's half-siblings, not full siblings, matching the half-sibling relationship already documented on Wayne's own page. Rachel's full siblings — children of William and Treva — were Arnold C. Morris (1918–2009), Donald Ellsworth Morris (1924–1924, also an infant death), and Thelma Lucille Morris (1926–1926, also an infant death, already documented as Wayne's half-sister on his own page).

Rachel died 20 October 1917, not yet two months old. Her death certificate records the cause simply as sudden death, certified by the acting coroner rather than an attending physician — the certificate's attending-physician section is left blank, suggesting no doctor had been treating her before her death. Part of the cause-of-death entry is not clearly legible and is not transcribed here rather than guessed at. She was buried three days later at what her certificate records as "Vrooman Cemetery."
