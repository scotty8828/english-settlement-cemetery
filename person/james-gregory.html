Biography

James Gregory was born in 1789 in Lancashire, England, and worked as a stonecutter and stonemason. In 1810 he married Elizabeth Harrison (1794–1881) — daughter of Jane Inskip Harrison and Benjamin Harrison, already extensively documented on this site as the family that founded the English Settlement. That marriage makes James a son-in-law of the settlement's founding matriarch, alongside Richard Morris, Eliab Sedden, and Henry Wheatall.

James and Elizabeth had six children, the eldest five born in Lancashire: Jane (1814), Christopher (1820), James (1823), Benjamin (1825), and Joseph (1830). The family emigrated to New York around 1826–1830, and their youngest child, Thomas — recorded in family records simply as "Doctor Thomas Gregory," suggesting he went on to practice medicine — was born there in 1832. The family later settled in Rome Township alongside the rest of the Harrisons.

James died 1 March 1863 in Crawford County, Pennsylvania, and was buried at what is now English Settlement Cemetery.
