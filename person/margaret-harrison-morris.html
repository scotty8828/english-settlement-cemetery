Biography

Margaret Harrison was born 2 August 1810 in Blackburn, Lancashire, daughter of Richard Harrison and Mary Ann Malley Harrison — both already documented on this site, Richard being one of Jane Inskip Harrison's original nine children.

Margaret married James Morris (1813–1846), son of James and Ann Morris and brother of Richard Morris. Family tradition holds that James died while working in Pittsburgh; their daughter, Elizabeth Ellen Morris (1845–1906), was born there the year before his death and later married into the Hopkins family.

Margaret died in 1854, the same year as her mother, eight years after her husband. The exact date and cause of her death are not yet known.

Ancestry-indexed Lancashire baptism records possibly matching Margaret and Jane show them baptized at two different chapels (Blackburn and Hoghton) rather than together — worth noting as mild supporting evidence that they weren't twins, though these are common enough names in Lancashire parish records that the identifications themselves aren't certain. The master cemetery record lists Margaret as not confirmed buried here, despite her husband and mother both being documented burials — worth checking whether she's actually here under a slightly different record, or buried elsewhere. Her exact death date is not known, only the year.
