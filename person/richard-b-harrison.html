Biography

Richard B. Harrison was born 4 February 1848 in Rome Township, near Vrooman, a son of John Brown Harrison (1824–1905) and Ellen Harrison Harrison (1828–1911), both already documented on this site. He had at least one sibling, Eleanora Harrison Morris (1849–1925), not buried here.

Richard never married. According to his 1930 obituary, he spent practically all of his life in the immediate vicinity of where he was born, and was held in high esteem by a wide circle of acquaintances.

In his final year or more of life, in failing health, Richard left Rome Township and made his home in Spartansburg with his nephew Leon Morris — Eleanora's son — and other relatives. Even so, he remained active and "around as usual" until he was stricken by a stroke on a Sunday evening at about seven o'clock. He died a few days later, 19 February 1930, at three o'clock in the afternoon, at Spartansburg, at the age of 82.

Funeral services were held from the home of Mr. and Mrs. Leon Morris in Spartansburg, with Rev. A. W. Gibson of the Spartansburg M. E. Church officiating. Interment followed at Harrison Cemetery near Vrooman.

Obituary
Titusville Herald · 20 February 1930, p. 10

ROME TOWNSHIP PIONEER DIES AT SPARTANSBURG — Richard B. Harrison, Aged 82 Years, Succumbs to Stroke Sustained Sunday Evening — Richard B. Harrison, life-long resident of Rome township, but who has spent the past year or more with relatives in Spartansburg, passed away in that village at 3 o'clock yesterday afternoon, following a stroke sustained at about 7 o'clock Sunday evening. Although Mr Harrison had been failing in strength for some time, he was around as usual until stricken on Sunday. Mr Harrison was 82 years of age and was born in Rome township, near Vrooman, son of John and Ellen Harrison. He never married and spent practically all of his life in the immediate vicinity of where he was born, enjoying a large acquaintance among whom he was held in high esteem. Since going to Spartansburg he had made his home with Mr and Mrs Leon Morris and other relatives. Funeral services will be conducted from the home of Mr and Mrs Morris at 11 o'clock Friday forenoon, with Rev A W Gibson of the Spartansburg M E church officiating. The interment will be made in the Harrison cemetery near Vrooman.

Age at death is well corroborated: obituary, Find A Grave, and his documented birth/death dates (4 Feb 1848 – 19 Feb 1930) all agree on 82 — no conflict, unlike some other family members' records.

The obituary's own headline misprints his name as "Richard P. Harrison" before correcting to "Richard B. Harrison" in the body — noted here rather than silently smoothed over, since it's an error in the original source, not a real identity question.

His death certificate exists on Find A Grave but the scanned image is not clearly legible; the account of his final illness above is drawn from the obituary rather than the certificate itself. Worth a closer look at the original certificate image if a sharper copy becomes available.
