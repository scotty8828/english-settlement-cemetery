Biography

James L. "Jimmy" Morris was born 27 April 1848 in Pittsburgh, Allegheny County — notably not in Crawford County, unlike most of his family already documented on this site. He was a son of William "Billie" Morris Sr. (1811–1890) and Jane Harrison Morris (1818–1885), both already documented on this site. He had at least six siblings, most already documented here: William S. Morris Jr. (1837–1915), Mary Jane Morris Sedden (1846–1930), Benjamin Harrison Morris (1852–1901), John Brown Morris (1855–1926), and Samuel Morris (dates uncertain in the cemetery's own record, though Find A Grave gives a death year of 1853). Two further siblings, Sarah Ann Morris Myers (1839–1906) and Nancy Elizabeth Morris Magee (1844–1874), are not currently found in the cemetery's own record.

On 17 October 1870, at the home of her father James A. Vroman, James married Elmira Amelia Vroman (1849–1915), also already documented on this site. They had three children — Ruby, Reuben, and Alfred, all already documented on this site — and James outlived every one of them: Alfred died in childhood in 1886, Elmira herself died in 1915, Reuben died in 1917, and Ruby, the last of his immediate family, died in 1919.

James spent practically all of his life in the vicinity of Vroman, where his obituary says he "was held in high esteem." He died 2 April 1921 at the Titusville City Hospital, following a long illness of which the final two weeks were spent in the hospital. This is the first documented hospital death — as opposed to a death at home — among everyone built on this site so far, and marks a real shift worth noting: by the early 1920s, at least some members of this rural community had begun dying in a hospital rather than in their own homes, a change from every earlier death this site has documented.

Having outlived his wife and all three of his children, James's will — made in his final years — left his entire estate to his two grandsons, Harold and Gerald Morris, sons of his late son Reuben, "share and share alike," with the Titusville Trust Company appointed guardian of their inheritance until they came of age. Funeral services were held at the United Presbyterian church at Vroman, with burial following at what his obituary calls "the Vroman cemetery" — another data point among many already documented on this site for the cemetery's several historical names.

Primary source — obituary, Titusville Herald, 4 & 7 April 1921

"James Morris died at the Titusville City Hospital Saturday afternoon following a long illness, two weeks of which he had been in the hospital. Mr. Morris was born in Pittsburgh on April 27, 1848, so he was 72 years, 11 months and 2 days of age when the end came. He was married to Almira Vroman on Oct. 17, 1870, at the home of the bride's father (James A. Vroman). They had three children, one who died in infancy, Reuben who died five years ago and Ruby whose death occurred about a years ago. Mrs. Morris died about five years ago. There are two grandchildren, besides a sister and brother, John B. Morris of Rome Township and Mrs. Mary J. Sedden of near Vroman surviving.

Mr. Morris spent practically all of his life in the vicinity of Vroman, where he was held in high esteem. Services in memory of James Morris were conducted from the United Presbyterian church at Vroman Tuesday at 2 p.m., Rev. H.L. Grimes, of this city, officiating. There was a goodly attendance of relatives and friends and numerous floral tributes. Interment was made in the Vroman cemetery, the pall bearers being Stewart Paul, F.M. Fish, George and Fred Farrington."

— Titusville Herald, 4 & 7 April 1921, as reproduced on Find A Grave. The paper's "Almira Vroman" is a spelling variant of Elmira, consistent with variants already noted on her own page and their son Reuben's.

Last will and testament
Crawford County Will Books
"I, James Morris, of Rome Township, County of Crawford, and State of Pennsylvania, being of sound mind, memory and understanding, do make and publish this my last Will and Testament, hereby revoking and making void all former Wills by me at any time heretofore made.

First. — I give devise and bequeath all my property, real, personal and mixed, wheresoever situate, to my two grandsons, Harold Morris and Gerald Morris, children of my deceased son, Reuben Morris, share and share alike, To them and their heirs and assigns, forever.

Second. — I do appoint that the Titusville Trust Company, of Titusville, Pennsylvania, shall have the Guardianship of the estates hereby devised and bequeathed to the said Harold Morris and Gerald Morris during their minority, and shall hold all of said property for them during that time."
