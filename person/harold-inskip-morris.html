Biography

Harold Inskip Morris was born 2 February 1907 in Rome Township, one of twin sons of Reuben Morris (1875–1917), a farmer, and Anna Morris (1879–1954). Reuben and Anna were first cousins; Anna's father was Inskip Morris, whose name Harold carried as his middle name. The state birth certificate, filed 13 February 1907, was signed by Dr. E. E. Maitland of Spartansburg. It gives Reuben's age as 31 and Anna's as 27, and records six children living for this mother. That matches the four children Anna had earlier had with Patrick Dailey, plus the twins. His twin brother was Gerald James "Gerry" Morris (1907–1944).

Harold was ten when his father died in September 1917. The unusual history of his mother's marriages is told on her page.

On 1 May 1923, at Jamestown, New York, Harold married Magdelene Clara Smith (1905–1930) of Meadville, known as "Peg." Witnesses were Mrs. Maude Dailey and Ethel A. Eddy, and the record was filed on 25 June 1923. The groom's entry gives his residence as Meadville, his trade as moulder, his age as 22, and his parents as Reuben Morris and Anna Morris. He was in fact 16: his birth certificate gives 2 February 1907. The form has a section for a parent's written consent when a party is a minor, and it is blank. A stated age of 22 would have avoided needing one, but the record does not say why the age was given.

Find A Grave lists four children as Harold's: Granville James "Granny" (1923–2011), Donna Gloria (1925–2008), Ronald DeWitt "Jack" Clark (1930–2017) and Duane Harold (1935–1991). Which wife was the mother of each is not shown there. Peg died in 1930, and Ronald, who carries the surname Clark, was born that year; the circumstances have not been documented. In 1933 Harold married Alice Nora Tranmel (1901–1999), the date as given on Find A Grave; the marriage record itself has not been seen.

By 1937 the couple lived at 1011 Walnut Street, Erie. Harold's death certificate gives his trade as moulder, with ten years in the work, and the industry as "Erie Malleable." His half-brother Martin Dailey was also recorded as a moulder in 1917. Harold died on Friday, 12 March 1937, at Hamot Hospital in Erie, at 30 years, 1 month and 10 days. The cause was lobar pneumonia, and the physician had attended him from 10 March. His wife Alice is named as his wife, and his mother, then "Mrs. Anna Daily" of 632 South Franklin Street, Titusville, signed as informant. He was buried on Monday, 15 March 1937. His twin Gerald died seven years later, and their mother outlived them both.

His age on the 1923 license: The record gives Harold as 22 on 1 May 1923. His birth certificate and Find A Grave both give 2 February 1907, which made him 16. His half-brother Martin Dailey's 1917 record (age given as 21, probably 19) overstates age in the same way, which lends support to the view that Find A Grave's 1898 birth year for Martin is right and the record's age is wrong. The handwriting on the 1923 form is difficult, and the bride's age was not read.
Parents documented: The birth certificate, death certificate and 1923 marriage record all name his parents as Reuben Morris and Anna Morris, so the father's identity does not rest on family tradition. The certificates also place Anna's later surname: on 12 March 1937 she signed as "Mrs. Anna Daily," so she was using Dailey by then.
The twin: The birth certificate marks the birth as twins, but the entry for order of birth is hard to read, so it is not known which twin was born first. Gerald's own record has not been seen.
Name of the cemetery: The death certificate gives the place of burial as Titusville, Crawford County, and the date as 15 March 1937. Find A Grave and the master record place him at Harrison Cemetery. Local records of the time call this ground Vrooman or Maple Grove; the museum treats these as names for one burial ground.
Children and wives: The children's mothers, Ronald Clark's place in the family, the 1933 marriage and the burial places of Peg and Alice are not yet documented here.
