Biography

Mary Ellen Vroman was born 2 October 1846, the daughter of James A. Vroman and Mary Ellen Harrison Vroman — and so a granddaughter of Richard Harrison, one of the three founding heads of the English Settlement. Her parents had married on Christmas Day 1845 at Maple Grove, Rome Township, in a double wedding alongside her aunt Elizabeth Harrison and Henry Wheatall — an event described in a family letter documented elsewhere on this site. Mary Ellen had at least one sibling, a younger sister, Elmira Amelia Vroman, who would go on to marry into the Morris family herself.

Mary Ellen married George W. Owens, though no record of the date has yet been found. They had a son together, James L. Owens, born in 1871.

Mary Ellen died 29 March 1871, at 24 years old. Her son James died the following year, before his first birthday, and her husband George died in 1877 at just 30. The timing of James's birth and Mary Ellen's death in the same year raises the possibility that she died from complications related to childbirth, though no record here states a cause of death directly.
