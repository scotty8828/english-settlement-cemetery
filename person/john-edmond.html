Biography

John Edmond was born 19 May 1801 in Barony, near Glasgow, in Lanark, Scotland. He later emigrated and spent about three years in the mercantile business in New York City. There, in 1834, he married Isabella Nesbitt (1809–1875), herself a young widow — her first husband, Alexander Duff, had died in Elizabeth, New Jersey, leaving her with an infant daughter.

While still living in New York, John and Isabella purchased 200 acres of land in Rome Township, Crawford County, Pennsylvania. Rather than moving directly onto the land, the family relocated first to Pittsburgh, where they lived for about three years, before finally settling on their Rome Township property.

John and Isabella had six children together. Two are documented by name so far: Samuel Mason Edmond (1836–1915), who later served in the Civil War, and a son also named John Edmond. The other four have not yet been identified.

John died on 22 December 1847 in an accident while raising a beam — likely in the course of building on the farm he and Isabella had only recently settled. He was buried at what is now English Settlement Cemetery. Isabella outlived him by nearly three decades and later married a third time, to Benjamin Inskip Harrison.
