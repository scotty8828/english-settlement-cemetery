Biography

Thomas J. Crossley was born in 1844 in New York, the son of David Crossley and Jane Alston Crossley — a family already deeply woven into this project's story. David was himself brought to America as a boy by his grandmother, Jane Inskip Harrison, matriarch of the settlement's founding family, and would go on to become one of the earliest producers in the region's oil boom, drilling a well near the Drake Well itself in the winter of 1859–60. Thomas grew up alongside eight siblings, most of whom died young; only he and his brother James Price Crossley reached adulthood.

A letter written by David Crossley to a family friend in 1861 — during the opening months of the Civil War — reports that "Thomas signed the roll in the new company." That enlistment led to a commission: Thomas served as a Lieutenant in Company K of the 57th Pennsylvania Volunteers, according to both Ancestry.com veterans' records and his own Find A Grave memorial.

Thomas married Mary E. Baine, though no record of the date has yet been found. They had at least two children, Cora and David, both named as beneficiaries in their grandfather David Crossley's own 1880 will. Thomas died 15 September 1875 in Titusville, Crawford County, at 30 or 31 years old. Family tradition holds that he was killed by a train, though no documentary source has yet confirmed this account or given an official cause of death.

Additional biography will be added as research deepens. If you have information about Thomas J. Crossley — particularly documentation of his death, or details of his Civil War service — please use the contribution form below.
