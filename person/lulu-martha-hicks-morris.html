Biography

Lulu Martha Hicks was born 20 June 1888 in Rome Township, Crawford County, Pennsylvania, the daughter of George W. Hicks (1858–1949) and Martha Irene Hicks (1864–1948). Through her father, she was a niece of Clarissa V. Hicks Harrison and a grandniece of Timothy B. Hicks and Amy Young Hicks, all already documented on this site.

On 17 August 1904, at sixteen years old, Lulu married William E. Morris (1885–1966) in Corry, Crawford County. William was the son of Inskip Morris (1849–1930) and Mary Ellen Morris (1853–1922); his father was a brother of Edward Morris, Benjamin Morris, and John Morris, already documented on this site, making William their nephew.

Lulu and William had at least three children together: Opal Irene Morris Pier (1906–1979), Kenneth Wayne Morris (1907–1993), and Wayne E. Morris (1911–1911), already documented on this site, who lived only eighteen days.

Lulu Martha Hicks Morris died 15 July 1914 in Rome Township, at twenty-six years old, three years after the death of her infant son Wayne. No obituary or death certificate has yet been located for her; the cause and circumstances of her death are not yet documented on this page. After her death, William remarried and had at least four more children, two of whom — Rachel Louise Morris and Donald Ellsworth Morris — also died in infancy, and a third, Thelma Lucille Morris, who died the same year she was born; all three are documented on this site. William himself is buried not at English Settlement Cemetery but at Rose Hill Cemetery in Spartansburg, Pennsylvania.

No obituary or death certificate yet located: Unlike most pages on this site, no death certificate or obituary has been found for Lulu herself. Her death location and date are documented, but the cause of her death at only twenty-six is not.
Resolves William E. Morris's parentage: William was the son of Inskip Morris (1849–1930) — already numbered in the cemetery master record as a brother of Edward Morris, Benjamin Morris, and John Morris — and Mary Ellen Morris (1853–1922). This also resolves the identity of "Inskip Morris Jr. (1894–1901)" elsewhere in the master record: he was William's own younger brother, who died in childhood, not to be confused with his father.
Mother's birth year corrected: An earlier Ancestry tree view had shown Lulu's mother, Martha Irene Hicks, with a birth year of 1884; a more detailed Ancestry Facts page instead gives 1864, which is followed here as the more plausible figure for a woman whose daughter was born in 1888.
