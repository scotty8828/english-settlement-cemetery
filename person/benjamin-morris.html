Biography

Benjamin Morris was born 10 August 1840 in Pennsylvania, a son of Richard Morris (1810–1871) and Jane Harrison Morris (1806–1887), both already documented on this site. He was one of at least six children: Edward Morris (1831–1912), Pvt. James Morris (1837–1863), William S. Morris (1842–1891, not yet found in the cemetery's own record — a different person from William S. Morris Jr., 1837–1915, documented separately on this site, and worth keeping distinct), John Morris (1845–1928, page pending), and Inskip Morris (1849–1930, page pending).

In 1866, Benjamin married Lucy Ann Sedden (1847–1869), already documented on this site. She died young, in 1869. Their son, Frederick Wilson Morris, was born in 1867 — a birth year that helps settle a question left open on Iphigenia Jean Wheatall Morris's own page, where an Ancestry-sourced 1887 birth year for Frederick didn't fit cleanly with Lucy Ann's 1869 death. The 1867 date, drawn from Benjamin's own Find A Grave family record, fits the timeline properly.

In 1871, two years after Lucy Ann's death, Benjamin married Iphigenia Jean "Effie" Wheatall (1847/48–1912), already documented on this site. Together they had four children: Herbert Richard Morris (1883–1963), Rupert E. Morris (1885–1890, already documented on this site, who died in early childhood), Edna J. Morris Foley (1886–1941), and Clyde Nugent Morris (1891–1959). Iphigenia died in 1912; Benjamin outlived her by three years.

Benjamin farmed in the Vroman vicinity for the whole of his working life, and his obituary called him "a lifelong resident of the vicinity of Vrooman" and "a highly respected citizen." He was a member of the Grange. He died 22 November 1915 at 75 years old; his death certificate lists the cause as arteriosclerosis. His funeral was held at the home of his son Fred (Frederick), with burial following in the cemetery his own certificate records as "Vroman, Pa." — the same burying ground documented elsewhere on this site under its several historical names.

Primary source — Titusville Herald, 24 November 1915

"Benjamin Morse (sic Morris) of Vroman died Tuesday, aged 75 years, a lifelong resident of the vicinity of Vrooman, died at 12 o'clock Monday night. He leaves his wife, three sons Fred, Bert and Clyde Morse, all living at or near home, and one daughter, Mrs Eddy Foley of Pittsburgh. He was a member of the Grange and highly respected citizen. He leaves two brothers, John and Inskip Morse of Vrooman. The funeral will be held at the home of his son, Fred Morse at Vrooman Thursday afternoon at 2 o'clock."

— Titusville Herald, 24 November 1915, as reproduced on Find A Grave (contributed by Debra Orris). The paper's "Morse" throughout is a misprint for "Morris."

Monument

Obituary says "he leaves his wife" — almost certainly an error. Iphigenia, his second wife, died in 1912, three years before Benjamin, and his own death certificate marks his marital status as "Widower." No evidence anywhere reviewed of a third marriage. Left as a documented inconsistency, not corrected in the obituary text above.
Frederick Wilson Morris's dates likely resolved. Benjamin's own Find A Grave family record gives Frederick's dates as 1867–1938, which fits cleanly with his mother Lucy Ann Sedden's 1847–1869 lifespan. This replaces a conflicting 1887–1958 pair sourced from an Ancestry tree on Iphigenia's page, which never fit the marriage timeline. Iphigenia's page has been updated accordingly.
Two "William S. Morris" entries — not the same person. Benjamin's brother William S. Morris (1842–1891) does not appear in this site's cemetery record. He is a different person from William S. Morris Jr. (1837–1915), documented separately on this site — worth keeping distinct given the identical name.
Minor time-of-death discrepancy. The certificate records death at 10 P.M. on 22 November; the obituary describes it as "12 o'clock Monday night." Not resolved — likely just imprecise newspaper phrasing around the midnight boundary.
