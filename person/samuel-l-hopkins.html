Biography

Samuel L. Hopkins was born 2 November 1869, a son of Charles Hopkins (1843–1905) and Amanda Rossell Hopkins (1845–1902), both already documented on this site and buried here alongside him. He was one of at least eight children: Sophronia Desta Hopkins Martin (1867–1926), Herbert "Hub" Hopkins (1868–1944), Ida May Hopkins Brown (1869–1913, born the same year as Samuel — twins are a possibility, though not stated in any source reviewed), Leroy Hopkins (1875–1939), Lydia Hopkins VanGuilder (1879–1935), Eva Elvira Hopkins VanGuilder (1884–1958), and Angia Marthenia Hopkins Taft (1889–1918), none of them buried at English Settlement Cemetery.

Samuel's own death certificate records his birthplace as Michigan — which fits with a note already on his mother's page, that his older brother Herbert was also born there, suggesting the family lived in Michigan for a time before returning to Crawford County. Samuel's grandparents, Truman Hopkins (1814–1891, a documented military veteran) and Lydia Ann Sweet Hopkins (1816–1895), are also part of this cemetery's own record.

On 2 November 1899 — his own thirtieth birthday — Samuel married Effie Ann McDowell (1877–1943), also buried at English Settlement Cemetery, though her own page has not yet been built. Together they had at least seven children: Samuel Clyde Hopkins (1901–1976), Amanda Phyllis "Nanna" Hopkins Brown (1904–1993), Raymond Deforrest Hopkins (1910–1990), Philip Sheridan Hopkins (1913–1976), Wellington L. "Woody" Hopkins (1915–1985), Oakley Lamont Hopkins Sr. (1917–2012), and Rachel E. Hopkins Sutton (1919–2009) — none of them buried here.

His death certificate lists his occupation simply as "laborer," without further detail on what specific work he did.

Samuel L. Hopkins died 4 August 1928 at Tidioute, Warren County, Pennsylvania, at 58 years, 9 months, and 2 days old. His death certificate records the cause of death as suicide, by gunshot wound from a .25 caliber rifle, with "ill health" listed as a contributory cause. No further detail about the circumstances is documented in any source reviewed. His wife, Effie, was the informant on the certificate. He was buried 8 August 1928 at English Settlement Cemetery back in Crawford County, brought home despite having died in neighboring Warren County; the undertaker was John Reger of Tidioute.

Additional biography, including further detail on his working life and his family's time in Michigan, will be added as research continues. If you have information about Samuel L. Hopkins, please use the contribution form below.

Date of burial: 8 August 1928. Undertaker: John Reger, Tidioute.
