Biography

William Sedden was born 5 June 1834 in Paterson, Passaic County, New Jersey — the same town where the Morris family is believed to have settled for a time after landing in New York, before continuing on to Rome Township. William was the son of Eliab "Eli" Sedden and Jane Harrison Sedden, a granddaughter of the settlement's founding matriarch, Jane Inskip Harrison, through her son Richard Harrison — which made William a great-grandson of the family that helped establish the English Settlement.

William married Sarah Ann Morris on 15 November 1857. Sarah Ann was the daughter of William "Billie" Morris Sr. and Jane Harrison Morris, making her a granddaughter of James and Ann Morris — the same Morris family already documented at English Settlement Cemetery — and tying the Sedden and Morris lines together a second time, a generation after William's aunt Lucy Ann Sedden also married into the Morris family. William and Sarah Ann had at least one daughter, Clara J. Sedden, later Clara McCormick, born in 1859.

After William's death, Sarah Ann remarried John Bowker Myers in 1865 and had three more children with him. She died 6 June 1906 in Titusville, Crawford County, and is buried at Woodlawn Cemetery there, not at English Settlement.

William enrolled for Civil War service on 25 March 1864 at Meadville, Pennsylvania, joining Company A of the 2nd Pennsylvania Artillery. He was mustered in as a private on 29 March 1864. His enlistment record describes him at the time as a 28-year-old farmer, five feet five inches tall, with a fair complexion, blue eyes, and dark hair. On 22 April 1864 he was transferred to Battery G of the 2nd Pennsylvania Provisional Heavy Artillery.

William died on 18 August 1864, less than five months after enrolling, at the age of 30. Find A Grave lists him plainly as a casualty of the American Civil War, though the specific cause — wounds, disease, or another cause tied to his service — has not yet been documented; disease claimed far more Civil War soldiers than combat did, and no record so far indicates which took William. He was returned home and buried at English Settlement Cemetery alongside his mother's family.

Additional biography will be added as research deepens. If you have information about William Sedden's military service or the circumstances of his death, please use the contribution form below.

This project's own master record hasn't yet had its "buried here" field checked for William — an unfilled entry, not an actual dispute. Find A Grave confirms his burial plainly as "Harrison Cemetery, Crawford County, PA," another name for this cemetery, so his page reflects that directly; the master record should be updated to match. Sarah Ann's parentage (daughter of William "Billie" Morris Sr. and Jane Harrison Morris) is confirmed via Find A Grave and connects William Sedden's marriage back into the core Morris family documented elsewhere on this site. No source yet gives the specific cause of William's death in service; the four-and-a-half-month gap between his transfer and his death is consistent with either combat or the far more common causes of Civil War military death, disease and illness in camp, but nothing here should be read as confirming either.
