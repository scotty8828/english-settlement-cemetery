Biography

Jane Harrison was born in Lancashire, England, in 1810, daughter of Richard Harrison and Mary Ann Malley Harrison — both already documented on this site. She shares a birth year with her sister Margaret, but the two were most likely not twins.

Jane married Eliab "Eli" Sedden (b. 1810). Their eldest son, William, was born 5 June 1834 in Paterson, New Jersey — meaning Jane's own family was still in New Jersey well after the wider Harrison family had begun settling Rome Township, and remained there at least through 1840, when another daughter, Margaret Ellen, was born in Essex County, New Jersey. The family eventually joined the rest of the Harrisons in Crawford County.

Jane and Eliab had at least six children: William (1834–1864); Margaret Ellen, who married a Farrington (1840–1907); Thomas (1845–1914); Lucy Ann (1847–1869); Jane, who married a Kerr (1850–1932); and Esther, who married a Ridgway (1857–1927). Lucy Ann's own story connects directly into the Morris family already documented on this site: she married one of Richard and Jane Harrison Morris's sons and died young in 1869, leaving an infant son, Fred, who was raised by his grandmother Jane Harrison Morris after Lucy Ann's death — a detail recorded in a family letter used elsewhere on this site.

Jane died 21 July 1855, one year after her mother and her sister Margaret.
