Biography

Millard Rieck was born 8 August 1897 in Crawford County, Pennsylvania, one of eleven children of David August Rieck (1865–1950) and Ella Morris Rieck (1872–1956). He died 30 October 1900, just short of his fourth birthday, and was buried at English Settlement Cemetery. The rest of the family's later home ground was elsewhere in Crawford County: Ella was eventually buried at Rose Hill Cemetery in Spartansburg, alongside David.

Ella's own detailed 1956 obituary, published in the Titusville Herald, states she was born 1 June 1872 "in Vrooman" — a real, named place in Crawford County, and one more data point consistent with "Vrooman" as one of this cemetery's several early alternate names.

Ella Morris Rieck was a daughter of William S. Morris (1842–1891) and Eleanora "Elnora" Harrison Morris (1849–1925), and — per an Ancestry family tree reviewed for this page — a granddaughter of Richard Morris and Jane Harrison Morris (1806–1887), already extensively documented on this site. If this line holds, William S. Morris (1842–1891) would be a previously undocumented son of Richard and Jane, and a brother to their already-known son John Morris (1845–1928) — not confirmed by any primary source reviewed here. This William S. Morris (1842–1891) is a different, unrelated person from William S. Morris Jr. (1837–1915), already documented on this site as the father of Jesse Herbert Morris (#072); the two should not be confused.

Millard's ten known siblings all lived to adulthood: William August Rieck (1895–1979), Mildred Eleanora Rieck Goodwill (1897–1992), Minnie Maude Rieck Vanderhoof (1899–1982), Ida Marie Rieck Miller (1900–1969), Anna Mae Rieck Baker (1902–1985), Vera Ruth Rieck Elliott (1904–2001), Bertha Belle Rieck Cornelius (1905–1987), John David Rieck (1912–1962), Arnett Fred Rieck (1915–1996), and Wave Elwin Rieck (1917–2009).

Additional biography — including the circumstances of Millard's death and why he alone among his siblings was buried at English Settlement Cemetery — will be added as further research continues. If you have information about Millard Rieck, please use the contribution form below.

Primary source — his mother's obituary, Titusville Herald, 16 July 1956

"Mrs Ella M Rieck, 84, formerly of Spartansburg, RD 2, and the widow of David Rieck, died Saturday morning at the home of her daughters, Mrs Clement Miller of Cambridge Springs, after an extended illness. A daughter of William and Elnora Harrison Morris, she was born June 1, 1872, in Vrooman. On Nov 28, 1894, she was married to David Rieck who died in September, 1950. She leaves the following children: William Rieck of Corry, Mrs Mildred Goodwill of Erie, Mrs Guy Vanderhoof of Sugar Grove, Mrs Clement Miller of Cambridge Springs, Mrs Dorus Baker of Edinboro, Mrs Charles Elliott and Mrs Wilbert Cornelius of New Brunswick, NJ, John Rieck of Port-au-Peck, NJ, Arnett Rieck of Dunellen, NJ, and Wave Rieck of West Spring Creek; 31 grandchildren; 52 great-grandchildren; and a brother, Clarence Morris of Centerville, RD 1. Friends may call at the Torrey and Hays Funeral Home in Spartansburg where the funeral service will be held at 2 pm Tuesday with Rev Cassius Armitage of Cobbs Corners Church officiating. Burial will be in Rose Hill Cemetery in Spartansburg."

— Titusville Herald, 16 July 1956, p.5, as reproduced on Find A Grave. Millard, who died in infancy 56 years earlier, is not mentioned — consistent with common obituary practice of the period.

Possible undocumented son of Richard Morris & Jane Harrison Morris (1806). An Ancestry tree places William S. Morris (1842–1891), Millard's maternal grandfather, as their son — which would make him a brother of their already-known son John Morris (1845–1928). Not confirmed by any primary source reviewed for this page; if confirmed, it should be added to Richard Morris's and Jane Harrison Morris's own pages.
Two distinct "William S. Morris" men — do not confuse. William S. Morris (1842–1891, this page) is unrelated in the sources reviewed to William S. Morris Jr. (1837–1915), already documented as Jesse Herbert Morris's father (#072). The five-year age gap and the "Jr." suffix distinguish them on the master record, but the names are otherwise identical.
Ella Morris Rieck's death year conflict. Her Find A Grave memorial and her own detailed obituary (dated 16 July 1956, describing a death "Saturday morning") both agree on 1956. The Ancestry family tree reviewed for this page instead shows 1958. The obituary is treated as more reliable here given its detail and internal consistency, but the conflict is not resolved.
Family's later cemetery. Millard alone among the Rieck children was buried at English Settlement Cemetery; his parents and all other siblings are documented elsewhere (Rose Hill Cemetery, Spartansburg, and various towns in Pennsylvania and New Jersey). No source reviewed explains why.
