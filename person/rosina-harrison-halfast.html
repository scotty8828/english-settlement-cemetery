Biography

Rosina Harrison was born 5 April 1853 in Rome Township, Crawford County, Pennsylvania, a daughter of William Harrison (1821–1894) and Sarah Harrison Harrison (1830–1887), both already documented on this site. William and Sarah were themselves first cousins — a relationship already established on this site through John M. Hasson's page. Rosina had at least two siblings: Mary Ann Harrison Hasson (1848–1936), who married John M. Hasson, also documented on this site, and Nancy Harrison (1851–1860), who died as a young child.

On 12 November 1894, at Spartansburg, Crawford County, Rosina married Richard Albert Halfast (1855–1907), already documented on this site — his second marriage, following the 1893 divorce that ended his first. The marriage placed Rosina within the same extended Harrison family already connected through several other pages on this site: her husband's marriage license names her as a daughter of William and Sarah Harrison, and her sister Mary Ann's own husband, John M. Hasson, is independently documented here as well. No children of Rosina and Richard's marriage have been found in any source reviewed; Richard's only known child, Richard Henry Halfast, was born to his first wife in 1884.

Richard died suddenly on Christmas Day, 1907, leaving Rosina a widow at 54. She did not remarry, and according to her own obituary, spent the remaining twenty-one years of her life near Vrooman, in the same part of Rome Township where she had lived essentially her whole life.

Rosina Harrison Halfast died 14 March 1929 at her home near Vrooman, following a long illness; her death certificate lists the cause as chronic myocarditis. She was 75 years old. Her occupation is recorded on the certificate as "housewife." The informant on her death certificate was Albert Morris of Spartansburg — his relationship to Rosina, if any, and any connection to the Morris family otherwise documented on this site, has not been confirmed. She was buried at English Settlement Cemetery; her own death certificate records the burial place simply as "Harrison," and her obituary calls it "Vrooman cemetery" — both consistent with the cemetery's several historical names already documented elsewhere on this site.

Obituary — Titusville Herald, 15 March 1929, p. 2

"Rome Township Pioneer Passes Away Yesterday — Mrs Rosina Harrison Halfast, Aged 76 Years, Spent Entire Life Near Vrooman — Mrs Rosina Harrison Halfast, aged nearly 76 years, passed away at her home near Vrooman yesterday morning at 10 o'clock, following a long illness. Mrs Halfast was born in Rome township and spent practically her entire life in that vicinity. She was a woman who always took interest in activities of the community and was well liked by all who knew her. Her husband, Richard Halfast, passed away about 21 years ago. She leaves one sister, Mrs Mary Hasson of Cambridge Springs, four nieces and one nephew. Funeral services in her memory will be conducted Saturday afternoon at 2 o'clock at the United Presbyterian church preceded by prayer service at the house at 1 o'clock. Rev A W Gibson of Spartansburg will officiate. Interment will be in Vrooman cemetery."

— Titusville Herald, 15 March 1929, p. 2, via Find A Grave (contributed by Rusty Davis)
Certificate of Death — Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, File No. 34410
County of Crawford, Township of Rome. Rozina Halfast, female, white, widowed. Date of death: 14 March 1929. Cause of death: "Chronic Myocarditis." Occupation: housewife. Birthplace: PA. Father: William Harrison, birthplace PA. Mother: Sarah Harrison, birthplace PA. Informant: Albert Morris, Spartansburg, PA. Place of burial: "Harrison." Filed 15 March 1929; registrar Robert A. Kerr.
— Pennsylvania Department of Health, Bureau of Vital Statistics, via Ancestry.com (contributed by Ted K on Find A Grave)

Age at death — minor discrepancy. Calculated from her documented birth and death dates, Rosina was 75 years old when she died. Her death certificate's own age field is difficult to read and may say something else; her obituary rounds up to "nearly 76" in the text and "76 Years" in the headline. Not treated as a real conflict, just a recording variance typical of the period.
Cemetery name — another documented variant. Rosina's death certificate records her burial place as "Harrison," and her obituary calls it "Vrooman cemetery" — both are consistent with the cemetery's several historical names already documented elsewhere on this site, alongside "English Settlement," the name recorded on her own husband's 1907 death certificate.
Informant's surname not investigated. Albert Morris of Spartansburg was the informant on Rosina's death certificate. Given the extensive Morris family research already on this site, this may or may not be a coincidence of surname — no connection has been confirmed.
