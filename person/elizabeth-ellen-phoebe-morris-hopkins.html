Biography

Elizabeth Ellen Morris was born 5 February 1845 in Pittsburgh, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, the daughter of James Morris (1813–1846) and Margaret Harrison Morris (1810–1854), both already documented on this site. Her father died in 1846, when Elizabeth was only about a year old, and her mother died in 1854, when Elizabeth was nine — she was orphaned before the age of ten. Her father was the brother of Richard Morris, one of the settlement's original 1834 landowners; her mother was a sister of Ellen Harrison Harrison, wife of John Brown Harrison, also documented on this site.

By about 1865, Elizabeth had married Leroy "Roy" Hopkins — already documented on this site — and settled with him in Crawford County. They had at least four children together: Richard Findley Hopkins (1865–1953), John Hopkins (1868–1935), Annie Margaret Hopkins Farrington (1870–1941), and William Leroy "Will" Hopkins (1874–1958).

Leroy died 23 March 1906. Elizabeth survived him by only about two months, dying 28 May 1906 as his widow. Her death certificate records the cause as "enlargement of heart causing dropsy," a condition present for about 120 days before her death — the same general kind of heart failure that had already taken her husband. She was buried, per her own death certificate, at what is there recorded simply as "Vroman" — one further primary-source instance of that alternate name for this cemetery. Her funeral was handled by the same undertaker, George W. Peck of Grand Valley, who had already buried both her husband and her brother-in-law Charles Hopkins within the previous ten months — three funerals for this one extended family within less than a year.

Given the two-source agreement elsewhere, this is treated as a likely clerical error on the death certificate rather than a genuine second name.
