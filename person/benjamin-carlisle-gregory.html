Biography

Benjamin Carlisle Gregory was born 8 February 1895, the son of William Valentine Gregory (1858–1933) and Martha Melvina "Bina" Conover Gregory (1858–1912). He died 12 January 1898, just under three years old, and was buried at English Settlement Cemetery. His headstone carries a floral relief carving above his name and dates.

He was one of four known children of William and Martha Gregory: his siblings were Alfred Garrett Gregory (1885–1965), Christopher Conover "Chris" Gregory (1887–1927), and Jesse Howell Gregory, who was born later in 1898, the same year Benjamin died.

Through his father's line, Benjamin was part of a family already deeply rooted at English Settlement Cemetery. His grandfather Benjamin Gregory (1825–1913) was the son of James Gregory (1789–1863) and Elizabeth Harrison Gregory (1794–1881), both buried here — and family-tree research places Elizabeth as a daughter of Benjamin Harrison (1750–1824) and Jane Inskip Harrison (1766–1845), the matriarch and patriarch of the cemetery's founding Harrison family. If that line holds, Benjamin Carlisle Gregory was a great-great-grandson of Jane Inskip Harrison — a connection not yet reflected on her own published page, which currently lists her known children as still under research.

Further biography — birthplace, cause of death, and confirmation of his place in the wider Harrison descent — will be added as research continues. If you have information about Benjamin Carlisle Gregory or the Gregory family, please use the contribution form below.
