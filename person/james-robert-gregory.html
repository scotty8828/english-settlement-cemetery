Biography

James Robert Gregory was born 14 June 1856, one of at least eight children of Doctor Thomas Gregory (1832–1915) and Elizabeth Ann Duff Gregory (1830–1899), both already documented on this site. "Doctor" was his father's given first name, not a professional title; the elder Gregory farmed in the "Windfall" district of Oil Creek Township for his working life. Through his father, James was a great-grandson of Benjamin and Jane Inskip Harrison, the settlement's founding couple.

James never married. As late as January 1915, at his father's death, he was still living at the family home — his father's obituary lists him simply as "James Gregory, living at home," the only one of the four surviving sons not settled elsewhere. By the time of the 1920 federal census he was head of his own single-person household in Rome Township, aged about 63, unmarried, with no occupation recorded.

By the later 1920s James was a resident of the Crawford County Home in Saegertown — the county's institution for residents unable to support themselves, standard for the period and carrying no further significance beyond what the records state. His death certificate lists his occupation as "inmate" of the "County Home," using the institutional terminology of the time, and his obituary in the Erie Times notes he had "been a patient for several years" before his death. He died there 21 January 1929 of organic heart disease, of about three years' duration — the same cause and duration recorded on his father's own death certificate fourteen years earlier.

The informant on James's death certificate was Myrtle Boyd, matron of the County Home, not a family member — consistent with his death occurring in an institutional setting rather than at home among relatives. He was buried at what his death certificate records simply as "Harrison" cemetery, alongside both of his parents.

Obituary
Erie Times · 31 January 1929 (via Find A Grave)

Word was received of the death of James Gregory, at the county hospital at Sagertown, Pa., Jan. 21st, 1929, where he had been a patient for several years, aged 68 years. He was a former resident of Rome Township, the son of Dr. T. and Elizabeth Gregory. Funeral services were held in the Hydetown M. E. Church.

Age at death — genuine conflict, not resolved. His death certificate records age 69. His obituary (Erie Times, 31 Jan 1929) states "aged 68 years." Calculated from his documented birth (14 June 1856) and death (21 January 1929) dates, he was actually 72. All three figures are given here rather than silently picking one.

Parentage listed as "unknown" on the death certificate — the informant, Myrtle Boyd, was the County Home's matron, not a family member, which explains the gap. Parentage is independently and consistently confirmed by his father's 1915 obituary, Ancestry family tree records, and Find A Grave.

Birthplace not directly documented. His death certificate lists it as unknown. No birth record has been found; his birthplace given above is inferred only from the family's documented residence in the area, not stated in any source.

Sibling Jane L. Gregory Millard's birth year is inconsistent between her parents' two pages on this site — 1866 on Thomas Gregory's page, 1868 on Elizabeth Duff Gregory's page. Not resolved here; flagged for a future correction pass on one or both of those pages.

Correction needed on Doctor Thomas Gregory's page: it currently lists James as "Not buried here." Find A Grave and Elizabeth Duff Gregory's own page both confirm burial at Harrison Cemetery. Should be corrected now that this page is live.
