Biography

Mary E. Baine was born in 1846. Her parents and place of birth have not yet been documented. She married Thomas J. Crossley (1844–1875), a Civil War veteran and son of David Crossley and Jane Alston Crossley, both already buried at English Settlement Cemetery. The date of their marriage is not yet known.

Thomas J. Crossley died 15 September 1875 in Titusville, Crawford County, leaving Mary a widow at about age 29. She survived him by nearly a quarter century, dying Friday, 11 August 1899 in Utica, Oneida County, New York — well over 300 miles from the family's home ground in Crawford County. Her remains arrived in Titusville by train on Sunday, 13 August 1899, for interment at English Settlement Cemetery, alongside her husband.

Mary was survived by two children: David A. Crossley, employed with the East Ohio Gas Company in Massillon, Ohio, and Cora H. Crossley, employed with the International Heater Company in Utica, New York. This David A. Crossley is a different, unrelated person from the David A. Crossley (1849–1860) already documented on this site — that David A. Crossley was a young son of David Crossley Sr. and Jane Alston Crossley who died in childhood decades earlier; this one was Mary and Thomas's adult son, living and working in 1899.

Thomas J. Crossley was a nephew of Ambrose Alston (1824–1898), also documented on this site, through Ambrose's sister Jane Alston Crossley. Ambrose's 1893 will named "the children of the late Thos. [P./J.] Crossley" as co-heirs, alongside Thomas's siblings James P. Crossley and Jennie Bowman, to two-thirds of his estate. Thomas J. Crossley had already died in 1875 — well "late" by 1893 — and Mary's own two children, David A. and Cora H. Crossley, are now documented by name, further strengthening the identification of Ambrose's will with this family, though no source yet directly names them as the will's heirs.

Additional biography — including her life between 1875 and 1899, her move to or connections in Utica, New York, and any children — will be added as further research continues. If you have information about Mary E. Baine Crossley, please use the contribution form below.
