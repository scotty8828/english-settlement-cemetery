Biography

Chester Lynn Heliker was born 28 April 1904 in Crawford County, Pennsylvania, the youngest child of Lester Elwin Heliker (1868–1923) and Lillie Serepta Bailey Heliker (1872–1939). He had four older siblings: Martin Van Buren Heliker Sr. (1889–1976), Enza Belle Heliker Morris (1891–1953), Elwin Clark Heliker (1893–1955), and Flossie C. Heliker Morris (1894–1963).

Chester fell ill on 12 March 1907. He died four days later, on 16 March 1907, at two years, ten months, and eighteen days old. His death certificate records the cause as cerebrospinal meningitis. He was buried two days after his death, on 18 March 1907, at English Settlement Cemetery.

A younger sister, Leona Fannie Heliker, was born to Lester and Lillie in 1912 and also died in early childhood, in 1913 — she is documented separately on this site.
