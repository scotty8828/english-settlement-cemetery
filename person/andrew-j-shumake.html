Biography

Andrew Jackson Shumake was born 8 February 1833 in Crossingville, Pennsylvania — corrected here from an earlier "New York" listing on Find A Grave, based on two of his own children's death certificates, which independently agree on Crossingville as his birthplace. He married Miranda A. Yost, and together they had at least three children: Cornelius Orvil Shumake (known as Orville), born 14 June 1858; Joseph Osborn Shumake, born 2 September 1861; and Carrie, who married into the Wheatall family — connecting the Shumakes to a family already extensively documented on this site through Bob Wheatall's own research.

Andrew was a member of two fraternal organizations, the Odd Fellows and the Freemasons — a common mark of social standing and mutual support in a 19th-century American community, though the specific lodges he belonged to have not yet been identified.

Andrew died 28 December 1880, at 47 years, 10 months, and 6 days old — an age recorded with unusual precision on his own monument. No cause of death has been found. He was buried at English Settlement Cemetery, a place his own memorial independently confirms was first known as Maple Grove — a third source, alongside Isabella Nesbitt Harrison's obituary and William B. Edmond's own memorial, to record that name.

Sources & notes
  • Find A Grave memorial #256813343 — birth, death, burial, spouse, and children; headstone photos by Laura Priester
  • Pennsylvania, U.S., Death Certificates, 1906–1972 — death certificates for sons Cornelius Orvil "Orville" Shumake (d. 1925) and Joseph Osborn Shumake (d. 1925), both naming their father as Andrew Jackson Shumake, born Crossingville, PA, and mother as Miranda Yost
  • 1850 U.S. Federal Census — Spring Township, Crawford County, PA, shows an Andrew Shumake, age 20, consistent with the family's presence in the area a generation before Andrew's own children were born
  • English Settlement Cemetery master record
Andrew's birthplace is corrected here to Crossingville, Pennsylvania, based on his sons' own death certificates, which independently agree with each other — this replaces an earlier "New York" listing sourced only to Find A Grave. His full name, Andrew Jackson Shumake, and his wife's maiden name, Yost, are likewise taken from these death certificates. Son Orville's full name was Cornelius Orvil Shumake, and his birth year is corrected to 1858 (from 1857) based on his own death certificate. Andrew's memorial independently states that this cemetery "was first called Maple Grove" — a third confirmation of the name, alongside Isabella Nesbitt Harrison's 1875 obituary and William B. Edmond's own memorial. No cause of death is recorded for Andrew himself.
